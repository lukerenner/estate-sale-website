Vintage Hermès "Le Timbalier" square silk scarf (carré 90) designed by Marie-Françoise Héron, first issued in 1961, reissued in 2001. This is the 2001 reissue. The design centers around a _timbalier_ (a military kettledrummer on horseback) elaborately festooned with ornate ropes, tassels, and ceremonial drum blankets.

Unused with original Neiman Marcus tag attached. Pristine vintage deadstock item showing no signs of wear. The colors are bright and bold. Also comes with box, box shows wear.

Carre 90, Measures approximately 34" x 35"

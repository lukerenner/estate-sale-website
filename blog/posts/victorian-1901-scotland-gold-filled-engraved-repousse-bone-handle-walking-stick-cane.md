Victorian era gentleman's walking stick featuring an L-shaped natural bone handle with a gold plated repousse collar showing and engraved commemorative inscription that reads

'Presented to D McKail by a Few Workmen in Abercorn Works Portobello 2/71901'

The Abercorn Brick and Tile Works was a historically prominent industrial site located in Portobello, Edinburgh, Scotland. Founded at the turn of the 19th century, it specialized in salt-glazed clay goods, bricks, and earthenware before eventually transitioning into industrial glass manufacturing later in the 20th century.

This walking stick would have been a highly valued item in the Victorian age as a gift from peers or workers to commemorate a significant milestone.

Measures 35 1/2" x 4 1/2" x 3/4"

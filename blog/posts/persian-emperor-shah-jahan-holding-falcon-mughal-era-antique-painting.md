Mughal period painting of the fifth emperor, Shah Jahan ****(reigned 1628–1658), representing the height of Mughal miniature art, combining Persian line work, Indian color palettes, and European-influenced realism in paint, ink, and gilding on vellum. Shah Jahan holds the falcon Baaz in his left hand. Dating between late 17th century to early 18th century.

The painting is under consignment for an estate. It was brought in framed. Photos of the front and back of painting were taken by the estate before framing, as shown. This painting was purchased by the estate from RoGallery in Long Island, NY.

Frame measures 12" x 15"

Painting measures approximately 5" x 8".

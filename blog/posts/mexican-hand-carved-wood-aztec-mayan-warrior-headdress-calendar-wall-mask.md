Hand carved wooden Mayan calendar wall mask, a popular piece of Mexican art hand crafted from cedar and local woods.

Features a stylized Mesoamerican warrior or deity wearing a traditional headdress. Prominently displays a circular ring etched with symbols or glyphs inspired by the Mayany calendar system or the Aztec sun stone.

Inside the calendar circle is a carved relief of a kneeling figure, a classic depiction often representing a calendar "year bearer," scribe, or deity in Mesoamerican codices.

Profile carvings of other faces and stylized animals are built into the outer edges of the headdress.

Measures 15 1/2" x 9" x 1 1/4"

Excellent condition.

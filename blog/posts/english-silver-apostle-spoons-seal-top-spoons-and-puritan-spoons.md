Gary Germer reveals an amazing collection of silver spoons found in a hoarder house located in a small town in Oregon. This silver is over 400 years old! They were purchased from Sotheby's London and other major auction houses in the 1970's - 1980's by Dr. Peter Cannon. After his death, the family found these items hidden all over the house, many times in their original packaging from the 70's with the receipts and catalogs!  These spoons often retail for thousands of dollars and some in the 6 figures; we have 17!  These spoons are just some of the amazing silver items found.

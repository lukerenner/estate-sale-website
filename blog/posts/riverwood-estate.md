The regal Riverwood Manor Estate Sale is a Dunthorpe waterfront masterpiece full of even more masterpieces. The home is stocked with exquisite works of art from paintings to bronze sculptures. Top quality furniture to match the multi-million dollar estate from solid wood sideboards and tables to custom made chairs and sofas. Executive desks. Not to mention the designer patio furniture. High end oriental rugs from hallway runners to enormous palace size tapestries. A kitchen that is full of everything in an entertaining party host's dream. Asian antiques from jars, sculptures, and china. A library full of books. Numerous flat screen televisions and stereo systems. Elliptical machines, outdoor equipment, and other sporting goods. An outdoor smoker with pellets. Lots of linens and bedding. Toys and games. A room full of Christmas and holiday decor. Plus a giant walk in closet overflowing with vintage clothing and accessories.

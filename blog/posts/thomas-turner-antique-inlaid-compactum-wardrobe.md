A magnificent wardrobe of considerable size, richly ornamented and exceptionally well crafted. Designed by renowned furniture maker Thomas Turner, this piece has no shortage of storage space, featuring four drawers, three cabinets of varying size, and two niches. To the left, a full length mirrored door opens to reveal a hanging rod and an array of gold hooks. A central square cabinet conceals a shelf, and several other shelves can be found behind the final cabinet door, which is fitted with an oval mirror framed by delicate floral motifs and scrollwork. Below the finely carved swan neck pediment is a small alcove with a charming turned spindle gallery. Embellished with elaborate marquetry and boasting an impressive silhouette, this compactum wardrobe is a truly marvelous find.

The lovely escutcheons appear to be strictly decorative. No key is necessary (or included); simply turn the pulls and open the doors!

H: 102" / W: 79" / D: 20"

This is an antique item that shows some minor signs of wear and tear, but overall it is in fantastic condition, especially considering its age! The latch on the far right door is somewhat faulty, but the door doesn't seem to have any issue with opening or staying shut.

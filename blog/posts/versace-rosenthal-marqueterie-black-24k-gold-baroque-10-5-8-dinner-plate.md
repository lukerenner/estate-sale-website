Versace and Rosenthal 'Marqueterie' porcelain dinner plate featuring an ornate black, white, and gold-tone Barocco and La Greca pattern.

The collaboration between the German porcelain manufacturer Rosenthal and the Italian fashion house Versace began in 1992 under the vision of Gianni Versace, with the first collections launching in 1993.

The Marqueterie collection features an intricate geometric and neoclassical scheme characterized by a 24k gold accented pinstripe and acanthus leaf cartouches.

Like all authentic Rosenthal meets Versace tableware, the porcelain pieces are produced in Germany, blending Versace's opulent fashion codes with precision porcelain craftsmanship

Measures 10 5/8"

Excellent condition, no chips, cracks, or repairs.

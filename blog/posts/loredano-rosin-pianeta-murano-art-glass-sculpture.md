This breathtaking sculpture by renowned Murano glass artist Loredano Rosin is a window into the cosmic. The ovoid form was crafted using the rare chalcedony glass technique, in which various mineral oxides are added to glass mixtures fused under high temperatures. The results are remarkable coloration effects like those seen in this sculpture. Here, the vivid cobalt and striations of gold are reminiscent of planetary nebulae, with a transparent teardrop form suspended in the center. The back of the sculpture is speckled with opaque pale blue glass, giving it the appearance of a precious stone. Extraordinary from every angle, this sculpture is sure to be the decorative highlight of any room!

Loredano Rosin was an Italian sculptor highly regarded for his stunning Murano glass works. Beginning his career at only age thirteen, Loredano Rosin would go on to achieve international fame, exhibiting his works around the world. Loredano and his brother Dino are credited with reviving and modernizing the Calcedonia glass process, a centuries old Murano tradition. Though Loredano passed tragically and unexpectedly in 1991, his legacy lives on in exquisite works like this one.

Antique Sawankhalok style large Celadon bowl, originating from Thailand. This piece is in the style of 14th to 15th century Sukhothai era pottery, but I believe it to be a 19th century reproduction.

Organic stoneware bowl showing signature thick, blue-green glaze and delicate under-glaze incised patterns.

Has repair at rim and minor wear to glaze, as photographed. Overall, I would consider this in excellent condition for old age. Please review photos.

Measures approximately 11" x 3 3/4" tall.

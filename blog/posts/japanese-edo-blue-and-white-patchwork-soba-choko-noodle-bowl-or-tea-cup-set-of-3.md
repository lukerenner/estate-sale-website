Featuring a hand painted traditional checkered or patchwork (ichimatsu) design

Versatile handle-less ceramic vessel traditionally used for soba dipping sauce and later for tea or sake. Would also make a nice little pot for your lucky bamboo.

Each slightly vary in size, but are approximate 2 1/4" tall x 3"

Excellent condition, no cracks or repairs. Mild wear to glaze, typical for age.

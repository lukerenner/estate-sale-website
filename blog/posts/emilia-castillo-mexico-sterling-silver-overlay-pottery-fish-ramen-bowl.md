Emilia Castillo "Plata Pura" sterling silver overlay ceramic fish bowl complete with a built in chopstick holder and wood chopsticks.

Handcrafted in Taxco, Mexico, these highly collectible mid-century pieces showcase Castillo's signature technique of fusing pure silver overlay designs onto glazed porcelain.

Off white tone bowl in stylized fish form showing sterling overlay gills and tiny fish.

Matching wooden chopsticks each include a silver cap each end adorned with small hanging silver fish charms.

Bowl measures 5 1/2" x 4 1/2" x 3 3/4" tall. Chopsticks measures 9 3/8" long.

Marked 'Emilia Castillo Plata Pura Mexico M.R. 16'

Excellent condition, no chips, cracks, or repairs.

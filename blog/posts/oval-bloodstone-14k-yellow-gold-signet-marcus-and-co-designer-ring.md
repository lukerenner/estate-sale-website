Bloodstone, also known as heliotrope, is a distinct dark green gemstone characterized by speckled red flecks of iron oxide or jasper. This subtle, understated stone speaks volumes, making this beautiful signet ring for a minimalist jewelry type of person.

Bloodstone measures 1/2" x 3/8"

Ring size 6

Total weight 3.07 grams

Band marked Marcus 14k. Marcus & Co. was a high-end New York jewelry firm that operated from 1892 to 1960.

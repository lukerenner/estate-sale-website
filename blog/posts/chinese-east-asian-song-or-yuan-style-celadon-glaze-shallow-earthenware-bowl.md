Rustic Chinese bowl in Song or Yuan Dynasty style. This is a reproduction piece, most likely dating from the 19th century, created to capture the organic essence of ancient Chinese pottery.

Features a pale grayish-green or celadon monochrome glaze with subtle incised or fluted details along the rim. Prominent unglazed buff colored center stacking ring, often seen in kiln wares like those from South China or Southeast Asia, allowed potters to stack multiple dishes on top of each other inside the kiln during firing without them fusing together.

Measures approximately 10" x 2" tall.

Shows obvious wear, please review photos. Several hairlines and some rough edges, but overall pretty good, solid condition for old age.

'Big Wheels Keep on Turnin' by the renowned Western artist Oleg Stavrowsky (1927 - 2020) .

His work frequently focuses on the technical mechanics of the frontier—such as the intricate construction of wagons, stagecoaches, and tack—captured with fine detail and deep, rich color palettes. This is a prime example of his work.

Born in Harlem, New York, to Russian parents, Stavrowsky served in World War II and worked for decades as a successful commercial illustrator and graphic designer. After visiting the Cowboy Hall of Fame in Oklahoma City in 1969, he decided to completely pivot his career to painting Western themes full-time.

Rustic wood frame measures 30" x 36" x 3 1/2", painting measures 21" x 28"

This pair of "Laminett" mid century modern ergonomic easy chairs by iconic Swedish designer Yngve Ekström are testament to the timeless appeal of Scandinavian modern design. Crafted from laminated and molded veneer, these chairs were constructed with comfort in mind. The elegant silhouettes of the bentwood frames are functional as well as stylish, meticulously engineered to provide the ideal seating curve. The upholstery appears to be the original suede. This mix and match set comes in two slightly different but complimentary colors, with one chair's upholstery being a darker purple tone and the other a blue tinged gray.

Yngve Ekström was a highly regarded Swedish furniture designer, known for his significant contributions to Scandinavian modernism. He and his brother Jerker Ekström founded ESE-möbler, a furniture manufacturing company that would later be renamed Swedese.

The Laminett chair is a variation on Ekströms most famous design, the Lamino armchair, which was named the Swedish furniture design of the 20th century by renowned interior design magazine Sköna Hem. The Laminett chair shares the Lamino's iconic curved frame, but has a more compact form due to its lower back.

Both chairs are stamped with "Swedese" and "Yngve Ekström."

H: 31" / W: 27.25" / D: 24"

These are vintage items that show signs of wear consistent with age. One chair has a stain on the back of the upholstery, not visible from the front.

Silver metal shows obvious surface scratching. Scuffs and surface scratching to wood shaft. Overall I would consider this in excellent condition for old age and an incredibly original collector's piece.

Measures approximately 36" x 1 3/4" x 1 3/8"

Lighter base reads "The Clark Lighter Platinum Electro Plate Pat June 27 1925"

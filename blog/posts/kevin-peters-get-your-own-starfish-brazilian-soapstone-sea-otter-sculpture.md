"Get Your Own Starfish" is the title of this endearing sculpture by renowned British Columbian artist Kevin Peters, whose remarkable works depict all manner of wildlife in exquisite detail. Carved from Brazilian soapstone, this stunningly lifelike sea otter is seated on painstakingly rendered coral and clutches a starfish. Peters' signature can be found near the otters' paw and on the bottom of the piece.

W: 14" / D: 7" / H: 12"

Good condition! Some small scuff marks near the tail. See photos for details.

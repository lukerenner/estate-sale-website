Large serving platter or charger plate designed by well known ceramicist, Ken Edwards from the El Palomar studio in Tonala Mexico.

Crafted in Tonalá, Jalisco, Mexico, this highly collectible high-fire stoneware piece belongs to the popular 'El Palomar Blue' collection. Showcasing iconic folk art elements, including hand-painted stylized blue birds, butterflies, and whimsical floral motifs against a textured pale blue clay background with earthy cinnamon tone highlights. Pretty to use as dinnerware or perfect for display as a centerpiece or on your wall.

Measures 11 3/4" wide x 1 1/4"

Excellent condition, no chips, cracks, or repairs.

Impressionistic and evocative, this beautifully framed oil painting by internationally acclaimed artist Barbara A. Wood depicts a somber seated woman with dark downcast eyes and clasped hands. "Green Stockings" features a color palette that is both vibrant and moody, with deep blues, muted pinks, striking red accents, and the titular sea foam green creating a pensive atmosphere. The vague environment and mystery shrouding the figure are certain to intrigue any viewer and allow this lovely work to blend seamlessly into a multitude of spaces.

This painting has an ornate gold-toned frame, with a gold plaque listing the artist and title at the bottom.

Raised in Ohio by her mother, her grandmother, and her great grandmother, Barbara A. Wood began painting alone in her room as a child, when frequent bouts of pneumonia left her bedridden. Frequently centering contemplative melancholic women and female family dynamics, Wood's work has been exhibited in solo shows internationally. Her paintings are in the collections of Elizabeth Taylor, Jane Seymour, Jerry Lewis, Mickey Rooney, Dick Van Dyke,  President George H. W. Bush, the King of Saudi Arabia, Dennis Weaver, Art Linkletter, Ron Howard, Gavin MacLeod, and many others.

Signed Brian Street BC Hand-Carved Yellow Cedar Burl Wood Bowl – Organic Live Edge Sculpture (#214)

Brian Street works out of the Bowser / Qualicum Beach area on Vancouver Island, BC, and exhibits through regional galleries such as the Salish Sea Market. Born in China and educated in Japan before settling in British Columbia, Street focuses on the natural movement, organic forms, and raw textures of salvaged Pacific Northwest wood. He frequently works with aged, twisted burls—often leaving natural live edges or enhancing the grain to highlight their "bird's-eye" figuring and color variations

Signed at base 'Brian Steet BC, Yellow Cedar, #214'

Measures 12 1/2" x 10" x 7" tall.

Excellent condition. Features natural wood characteristics inherent to burl timber, including natural fissures, bark pockets, and organic live-edge variations. Displays beautifully as a standalone sculptural centerpiece, table accent, or catchall.

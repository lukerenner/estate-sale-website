A spacious dresser from the Drexel Heritage Heirlooms Collection featuring a beautiful flame grain mahogany veneer and brass bail pulls. With its serpentine front and ogee bracket feet, this piece possesses an elegant silhouette that appeals to traditional aesthetic sensibilities.  Twelve drawers guarantee ample storage space, with plenty of organizational possibilities. Could serve as a "his and hers" dresser, or even be repurposed as a hallway credenza.

Drexel has been manufacturing high quality traditional style furniture in the American South since 1903. In 1951, Drexel acquired North Carolina company Heritage Furniture and became Drexel Heritage. Their Heirloom Collection speaks to their commitment to creating timeless, reliable pieces that will be treasured for generations.

H: 34" / W: 73.5" / D: 21"

This is a vintage piece that shows signs of wear and tear. There is some minor scuffing and chipping, particularly along the bottom of the dresser.

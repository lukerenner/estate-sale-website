This fabulously adorable dog themed bronze sculptural bowl is the work of accomplished sculptor Kwan Wu, best known for his large scale public works in China and the US.

Three meticulously rendered proud canines form the legs of the bowl, and two more perch on the rim with handles looped around their tails. The oceanic blue tone of the vessel is complimented by dazzling gold details, including the low relief depictions of various dog breeds found on the body of the bowl. An immensely endearing piece that appeals to dog lovers and fine art admirers alike!

H: 11"
W: 17"
D: 11"
Depth of bowl: 6"

Great condition. Some minor wear consistent with age. This work is quite heavy due to (bronze) construction materials.

Put your collectibles and treasured belongings on display in this elegant Chippendale style mahogany curio cabinet by Drexel Heritage, which features a mirrored back panel, thick glass shelving, and an interior light switch for optimal viewing! Showcasing the subtle beauty of traditional design, this piece stands on ogee bracket feet and is decorated with overlapping arch muntins. The cabinet is also banded with low relief decorative carvings beneath the molded cornice, and the ornate escutcheons are aesthetically pleasing as well as functional. A working key is included.

Drexel has been manufacturing high quality traditional style furniture in the American South since 1903. In 1951, Drexel acquired North Carolina company Heritage Furniture and became Drexel Heritage. Their Heirloom Collection speaks to their commitment to creating timeless, reliable pieces that will be treasured for generations.

H: 82" / W: 39" / D: 14.5"

This is a vintage piece that shows signs of wear and tear, though it is overall in good condition. There are some minor scratches and scuff marks on the wood.

With a serpentine front and cabriole feet, this Venetian style cabinet boasts a charming silhouette as well as gorgeous hand painted details. The front features a painting of a golden cup overflowing with fruit and flowers, signifying abundance and prosperity. Further floral ornamentation is found on each side, where bouquets have been painted with care, as well as in the row of blooms lining the bottom drawer. The cabinet opens to reveal ample storage space and a central shelf. Additional storage space can be found in the lower drawer.

H: 59"
W: 34"
D: 18"

Mostly in good condition, apart from one notable chip on the edge of the top. See photos for details.

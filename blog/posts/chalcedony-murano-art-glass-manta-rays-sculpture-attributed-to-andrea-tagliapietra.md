Featuring two delicate, vibrantly colored manta rays, this work of art is sure to be a centerpiece in any space. The vivid blue hues are particularly mesmerizing, and the meticulous attention to form is evident, creating the illusion that these creatures are truly gliding through water. Would make a particularly good decorative piece for a coastal home!

The remarkable coloration effects visible in this sculpture are the product of the Chalcedony glass technique, a centuries old tradition that involves adding various mineral oxides to glass mixtures fused under high temperatures. Be sure to place this sculpture near a window, where you can observe how these colors shift in the natural light.

The signature on the base resembles that of Andrea Tagliapietra, who is renowned for his mastery of chalcedony techniques and recognized worldwide as one of Murano's most talented glass makers. His sculptures of marine animals are particularly beloved, each one a precious work of art that speaks to Tagliapietra's talent, precision, and passion.

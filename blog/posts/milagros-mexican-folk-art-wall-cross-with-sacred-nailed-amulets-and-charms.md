Wonderful Mexican Milagros wall cross, a prominent form of Mexican folk art handmade by nailing dozens of small metal charms, or Milagros, meaning "miracles" in Spanish.

Hand carved wood cross with rounded edges. The surface is densely covered with small, stamped metal charms representing body parts, animals, celestial objects, sacred symbols, and more used for prayer and healing.

Measures 8 3/4" x 5 1/2" x 1"

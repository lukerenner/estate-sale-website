Incredibly sweet Vintage Ken Edwards Mexican pottery, cereal bowl from the El Palomar studio in Tonalá, Mexico.

Crafted in Tonalá, Jalisco, Mexico, this highly collectible high-fire stoneware piece belongs to the popular 'El Palomar Blue' collection. Showcasing iconic folk art elements, including hand-painted stylized blue bird, butterflies, and whimsical floral motifs against a textured pale blue clay background. The bowl is beautifully framed with a dark earthy brown rim.

Measures 2 3/4" tall x 6 1/4" wide

Excellent condition, no chips, cracks, or repairs.

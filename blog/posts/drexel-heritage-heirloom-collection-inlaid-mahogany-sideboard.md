From the Heritage Furniture Heirloom Collection comes this magnificent serpentine sideboard in the classic Hepplewhite style. With six dovetailed drawers, flame grain mahogany, bellflower inlay on tapered legs, and original brass hardware (including a spindle gallery), the craftsmanship and artistry of this piece cannot be overstated. Each drawer is of considerable depth, suitable for storing large amounts of dishware, and the center drawer is felt lined for silverware storage.

Drexel has been manufacturing high quality traditional style furniture in the American South since 1903. In 1951, Drexel acquired North Carolina company Heritage Furniture and became Drexel Heritage. Their Heirloom Collection speaks to their commitment to creating timeless, reliable pieces that will be treasured for generations.

H: 38" / W: 72" / D: 24"

Many scratches can be seen on the top of the sideboard. Otherwise in good condition, with no notable damages.

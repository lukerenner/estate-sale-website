A charming and versatile set of Georgian style mahogany bachelor's chests from the Drexel Heritage Heirloom Collection. Each chest features five drawers with original brass bail pulls and a hidden slide-out shelf. With their banded tops, serpentine silhouettes, carved scallop shell motifs, and ball-and-claw feet on cabriole legs, these chests exemplify the subtle beauty of traditional design. They would make a wonderful pair of nightstands, but could also be utilized as storage pieces in any room!

Drexel Heritage has been manufacturing high quality traditional style furniture in the American South since 1903. Their Heirloom Collection speaks to their commitment to creating timeless, reliable pieces that will be treasured for generations.

H: 27" / W: 28" / D: 17"

These are vintage pieces that show signs of wear and tear. The wood is chipped in some places, including at the top of one drawer and on one of the pullout shelves. (See photos.) Some small scratches are also visible.

Hermes Carre 90 100% silk 'Marché Flottant du Lac Inlé II' (The Floating Market of Lake Inle II) scarf in vibrant orange, red, yellow, turquoise and multi-colors. Designed in 2001 by Dimitri Rybaltchenko, this is an updated version of the 1998 original.

Features a vibrant bird's-eye perspective of the traditional floating markets on Inle Lake in Myanmar (Burma), featuring local merchants selling goods from long-tail boats.

Excellent condition, shows no signs of wear. The colors are bright and bold. Would make this an incredible statement piece or could be framed and hung for decor.

Carre 90, Measures approximately 34" x 35"

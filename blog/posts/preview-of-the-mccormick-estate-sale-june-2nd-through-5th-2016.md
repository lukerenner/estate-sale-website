The historic McCormick family has lived in this gorgeous home in St. Helens, OR for over 100 years. The name of McCormick bears a very close relation to the town of St. Helens, Oregon, for it was the advent of the McCormick lumber and milling interests into this locality which gave impetus to the growth of the town, they are one of the founding families of St. Helens and besides business interests, McCormick's have been valuable citizens of the community.

HOW IT WORKS
Our estate sales are FIRST COME FIRST SERVED. We do not have a sign up list. On the first day of the sale at 9:00 a.m. we'll hand you a number when you arrive to keep things orderly. Your number will be your place in line so you don't have to wait around outside. Be sure to return before the doors open at 10 a.m. We'll let in small groups of people at a time until everyone is happily shopping. Numbers are only handed out on the opening day of the sale. We accept cash, check or credit cards for payment.

Gary Germer & Associates is so excited to invite you to our Holiday Open House at our estate gallery in North Portland. We have been so busy working estate sales and unveiling our newest branch, The Roaming Emporium, that we are pleased to announce the reopening of our newly redesigned shop. To kick-off the holiday season, we have brought in a whole bunch of vintage and antique Christmas decor, including an exceptional collection of rare Christopher Radko ornaments. Along with the great holiday items available, our shop also is home to a variety of uniquely exquisite antiques, sterling silver, crystal, fine art, antiquities, vintage jewelry, art glass, clothing, furniture, and hundreds of home decor pieces perfect for gifting this season.

A lovely art nouveau style three bulb leaded glass lamp by early 20th century lamp manufacturers Duffner & Kimberly. The shade is a magnificent mosaic of striated pale yellow glass, which casts a cozy orange glow when the light is switched on. The rim of the shade also features accents of green, red, and orange, in an interlocking pattern of petal and diamond shaped glass fragments. The patinated dark metal base has a tapering stem and is ornamented with iris relief carvings.

New York based company Duffner & Kimberly formed in 1905 and began producing leaded glass and bronze lamps in 1906. Their mosaic style shades, construction materials, and high quality products have earned them frequent comparisons to renowned lamp manufacturer Tiffany Studios. Following the Panic of 1907, subsequent financial difficulties lead to the dissolution of Duffner & Kimberly, making their lamps treasured rarities today.

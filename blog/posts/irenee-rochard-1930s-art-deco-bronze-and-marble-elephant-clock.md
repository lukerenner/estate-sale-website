A spectacular three part art deco set attributed to French animalier sculptor Irénée Rochard, consisting of a clock centerpiece and two garnitures. Each piece is carefully crafted from marble and onyx, with sculpted gilt bronze ornamentation. The starring feature is the central ormolu elephant with its trunk raised in a dramatic curl. The square clock is gold toned, with the numbers on its face etched in a unique geometric font characteristic of the art deco style. The companion vases are rectangular in form and feature gilt bronze elephant heads, each with a looped rope or ribbon hanging from the end of its trunk. This extraordinary set is highly collectible and would make for a marvelous mantlepiece display!

Irénée Rochard (1906-1984) was a French sculptor who specialized in animal depictions, most often working in the bronze medium.

Includes original bell, pendulum, and key.

'Moocher' original limited edition bronze sculpture created by Oregon artist, David Manuel (1940) in 1986. Depicting a seated Native American woman offering an apple to a spotted pony with polychrome, cold-painted accents—specifically the bright red apples in the basket, the apple in the woman’s hand, and the distinct white and dark spots of the Appaloosa horse’s coat

Signed, titled, dated 1984, and limited edition 29/100 in bronze at base.

Attached to wood base with plaque at front showing title and artist name.

Measures 10 1/2" tall x 13" x 8"

Excellent condition.

This timeless ten drawer dresser by Leopold Stickley features dovetail drawers, elegant solid brass Chippendale pulls, and charming bracket feet. With solid, reliable construction and the ample organizational possibilities provided by its variety of drawer dimensions, this traditional dresser is an excellent choice for those seeking a bedroom storage solution.

Stickley is known for their high-end furniture and exceptional craftsmanship. Stickley traces its legacy to the tremendously influential Gustav Stickley, leading voice in the American Arts and Crafts movement, and his brother Leopold Stickley. In 1956, Leopold was named “Revered Dean of Cabinet Makers” by various magazine editors of The New Yorker, National Geographic, House Beautiful, and Fortune.

Drawers: H: 4.25" / D: 16.25"

Vintage Hermes Carre 90 100% silk 'Le Carnaval de Venise' (the Carnival of Venice) scarf in vibrant orange, red, and yellow multi-colors. Designed in 1993-1994 by Hubert de Watrigant, as signed in scarf. Showing a Venetian carnival scene of musical scores, masks and masked figures with subtle jacquard instrument weave.

Unused with original Neiman Marcus tag attached. This is a pristine vintage 90's deadstock item showing no signs of wear. It's bright bold colors make this an incredible statement piece or could be framed and hung for decor.

Carre 90, Measures approximately 34" x 34"

Venture up the Forest Heights and find the elegant Birkendene Estate Sale. This is a large home full of practically brand new furniture and fine art. The owner of the estate had recently moved in with all their treasures and values to be closer to family and purchased brand new furniture before a sudden passing. The home is full of like-new Restoration Hardware sofas, chairs, tables and other accent furniture, as well as some vintage McGuire and Baker pieces and other lovely things. Given how short the time they lived here, there is no real rubble to sort through, and everything is top notch and in great condition. Fine Art, including popular PNW artists Sally Haley and Charles Heaney. Chinese porcelains and Asian pottery. Vintage clothing and handbags. Toys, board and card games, and collectible dolls and accessories. Sporting goods, featuring a ping pong table and NordicTrack treadmill. Kitchen ware, pots & pans, dishes, and silverware. 

It is all brand new or tastefully curated items that simply haven't been lived in to their full potential yet. So let's celebrate their life and make sure all their amazing things find new life in your home!

Featured original paintings by Oregon artist: Carl Morris, Sally Haley, Arthur Rundquist, Louis Bunce, Charles Heaney, Eliza Barchus, Clyde Leon Keller, David McCosh, Hans Schiebold, Don Wilson, Rene Rickenbaugh

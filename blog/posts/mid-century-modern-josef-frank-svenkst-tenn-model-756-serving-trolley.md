With slender legs on casters, a double ended drawer accessible from both sides of the cart, and two fold out leaves, this charming serving trolley demonstrates the simple elegance and functionality of mid-century modern design. The lower tray provides additional storage space and is easily removable.

This versatile piece is made of veneered wood and was designed in the 1940s by renowned architect and interior designer Josef Frank for the Swedish company Svenskt Tenn.

Height: 26"
Depth: 31.5"
Width without leaves extended: 20.75"
Width with leaves extended: 35"

Some scuff marks visible on wood, including one small white mark on top and one on removable tray.

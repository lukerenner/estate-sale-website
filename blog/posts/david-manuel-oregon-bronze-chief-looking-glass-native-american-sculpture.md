"Chief Looking Glass," a signed polychrome bronze statue by David Manuel depicting a poised Native American chief. Manuel was born in Walla Walla, Washington and is best known for his bronze renderings of Native Americans and pioneers. His many public works and commitment to historical accuracy has earned him the title of official sculptor of the Oregon Trail and the official sculptor of the United States Marshals bicentennial. He currently lives and works in Oregon.

This figure stands with one hand clutching the barrel of a gun and is dressed in detailed colorful garb, with the utmost attention to historical accuracy. His contemplative expression and regal stance give him an air of authority and wisdom.

Signed by the artist and dated 2010 on the bronze base. Seated atop a wooden base with a plaque declaring the title and artist.

H: 32" / W: 12" / D: 8"

Good condition! No notable damages. This item is quite heavy due to construction materials.

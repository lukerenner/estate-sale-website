Bronze composite figurative statue of a cherub or Putto holding a dolphin. Modeled directly after an ancient Roman fountain statuette excavated at Pompeii. In original Roman gardens, these statues functioned fountain with water spouting from the dolphins mouth into a basin.

Measures 20" tall x 9 3/4" x 5 3/4".

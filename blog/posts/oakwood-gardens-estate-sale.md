Take a drive to the scenic Scholls countryside and find the magnificent Oakwood Gardens Estate, a storybook home renowned for its charming cottages, wedding barn, perfectly manicured grounds, and of course the oldest ALPACA farm in the country! The stunning estate is a decorator's dream sale that's filled with beautiful antique American oak furniture and classic transitional pieces, including a custom flat screen lifted entertainment console cabinet. Tons of artwork, framed prints, and other vintage decor from the differently styled estate cottages. Great antique Victorian lamps, shades, and modern lighting. Cultural pieces from Asian furniture and pottery to Peruvian weavings and other textiles. Fine Oriental and Persian rugs. Lots of collector china and tabletop ceramics. Sonos sound system. A complete home gym setup of high-end exercise machines and workout equipment. Even an extra large parrot cage!

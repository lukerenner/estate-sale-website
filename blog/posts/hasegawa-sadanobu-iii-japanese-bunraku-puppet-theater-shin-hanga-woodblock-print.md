Original early century, Japanese woodblock print by Hasegawa Sadanobu III (1881-1963), a prominent artist from the Osaka school of ukiyo-e printmakers

The image depicts a stylized Bunraku, puppet theater figure in robe. The artist is famously known for his detailed mid-century series titled “Illustrated Collection of Famous Japanese Puppets of the Osaka Bunrakuza.”

Original 1920's print with tissue attached to black matte. Print measures 15" x 10". Tissue is torn at upper edge and slightly wrinkled.

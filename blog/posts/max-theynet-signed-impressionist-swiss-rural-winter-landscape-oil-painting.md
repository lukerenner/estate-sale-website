Max Robert Theynet (Swiss, 1875-1949) early 20th century, impressionistic oil painting on canvas showing a serene Swiss winter landscape of a quaint rural home in blankets of snow and a prominent looming pine tree.

Gilt wood frame with matte measures 28 1/4" x 24 1/2", actual painting measures 22 1/2" x 19"

Excellent condition, minimal wear to frame.

As found on AskArt: Max Robert Theynet was born 1875 in Colombier, a small township in Switzerland. He was a representative of the Impressionist Painting and primarily active in Zuric. His motifs are mainly landscapes and floral still lives that are characterized by a bright and light range of colours.

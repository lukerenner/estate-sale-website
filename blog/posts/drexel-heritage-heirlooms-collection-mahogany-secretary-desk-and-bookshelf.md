Constructed from striking inlaid mahogany, this exceptional two part Chippendale style secretary desk by Drexel Heritage is a practical storage solution with a handsome silhouette. The desk stands on ogee bracket feet and features an elegant broken pediment top with a central shell shaped finial. The four lower dovetailed drawers are fitted with Queen Anne brass bail pulls. Lopers slide easily in and out to support the drop front desk, which opens to reveal a plethora of compartments, including eight drawers with dainty dutch drop pulls, pigeon holes, and a central cabinet flanked by two discrete narrow drawers that resemble fluted columns. Two candle slides are located above the desk component. The upper doors are embellished with an oval inlay and swing open to reveal multiple shelves, ideal for storing books. Both the drop front and upper cabinets are lockable, with a working key included.

This is a marvelous piece with significant organizational capacity, demonstrating the expert craftsmanship Drexel is known for.

Drexel Heritage has been manufacturing high quality traditional style furniture in the American South sincedesk 1903. Their Heirloom Collection speaks to their commitment to creating timeless, reliable pieces that will be treasured for generations.

H: 91.5" / W: 42.5" / D: 21"

Vintage Anri hand-carved wood wall relief plaque, titled "Madonna with Diadem" (Madonna and Child with Angel) designed by renowned artist Juan Ferrándiz.

An incredibly sweet, mid-century rendition of the divine mother and child handcrafted in Italy. Features the Virgin Mary (Madonna) with a radiant golden halo tenderly cradling the infant Jesus, while a small, smiling angel holds up a cluster of flowers. Hand-carved from alpine hardwood with beautiful hand-painted detailing and gold leaf accents. Excellent vintage condition and ready to hang!

Hole at back for hanging.

Measures 16 3/4" x 10 1/2" x 2 1/2"

Anri Italy at back.

Excellent condition, no damage found.

Crafted by master woodcarvers in Val Gardena, Italy, ANRI sculptures are highly prized by collectors for their delicate, expressive features, soft hand-painted finishes, and beautiful three-dimensional detail

A tasteful and timeless breakfront china cabinet with gorgeous mahogany inlay by Drexel Heritage, from their Heirlooms Collection. This large cabinet has an impressive silhouette, with a uniquely carved broken pediment on the top and deep moldings. Behind the four lower cabinet doors you'll find spacious concealed storage, including shelving and a central felt lined drawer, originally intended for flatware. Store china or collectibles in the upper glass cabinets, which are ornamented with fine latticework. They each feature four shelves for optimal organization. All cabinet doors are lockable, and three yellow tassel keys are included.

Drexel Heritage has been manufacturing high quality traditional style furniture in the American South since 1903. Their Heirlooms Collection speaks to their commitment to creating timeless, reliable pieces that will be treasured for generations.

H: 92.5 / W: 74.5" / D: 14"

Iconic Lalique France Champs Elysees crystal centerpiece bowl designed by Marc Lalique in 1951, son of founder Rene Lalique.

The glass is quite heavy and features a combination of beautifully frosted (satin-finished) and clear crystal elements to catch and diffuse light. The crystal leaves were inspired by the plane and horse chestnut trees lining Paris's farmhouse avenue. A stunning showcase piece set under lights.

Measures approximately 18" x 10" x 7 1/2"

Excellent condition, no chips, cracks, or repairs. Minor surface scratching a base.

Etched Lalique France hallmark at base.

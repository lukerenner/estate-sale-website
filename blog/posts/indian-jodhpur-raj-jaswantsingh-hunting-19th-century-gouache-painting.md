Indian Jodhpur region gouache painting, dated 1838 of Raja Jaswantsingh Hunting by artist Bhanwardas. The painting depicts Raja Jaswant Singh on horseback in mid-gallop, raising a curved sword to strike two wild boars. He is accompanied by spotted hunting hounds attacking the boars, several white hares scattered across the foreground, and three armed retainers peeking over a stylized rocky ridge in the upper left.

The painting is under consignment for an estate who originally purchased the painting in 2013 from RoGallery in Long Island, NY.

Frame measures 17" x 17"

Painting measures approximately 9 3/4" x 9 3/4",

Very good condition, bright, bold colors. There are 2 tears at the left side and a flaw in the paint in the white part of clothing. All as photographed.

Vintage Hermes Carre 90 100% silk 'Prieres au Vent' (Prayers in the Wind) scarf in vibrant orange, turquoise, and red multi-colors. Designed in 1996 by Dimitri Rybaltchenko, as signed in scarf.

Features a beautiful Buddhist-inspired motif depicting Tibetan pilgrims holding large prayer wheels at the corners, traditional prayer flags, and a central wind horse carrying wish-granting jewels over snow-capped mountains.

Seemingly unused and in excellent condition, shows no signs of wear. Comes with box. Box shows light wear. It's bright bold colors make this an incredible statement piece or could be framed and hung for decor.

Carre 90, Measures approximately 35" x 35"

The Westridge Estate Sale is an upscale home in a picturesque neighborhood full of the finest treasures. The lavish estate belonged to retired Doctor and collector of luxurious lifestyle living. High quality designer furniture from neoclassical to midcentury modern, light-ware sofas and chairs, including a hideabed in great condition. French antique clocks and armoires, a 19th century German Biedermeier silver cabinet, a very sexy art deco rosewood drink cabinet by John Richard Racasse, and a stunning Austrian artist hand tole painted queen bed trunk and matching decor. Patio and wicker furniture, planters, and pots. Dozens of Persian rugs. Exquisite fine art, from original oil paintings to elegant 18th century etchings ranging in period to modern lacquer frames. Christmas ornaments and decor. Plenty of garden equipment and tools. Sporting goods, workout equipment, and bags, including a mountain bike, snowshoe and skiing equipment. Tons of kitchen items from porcelain china, glassware, platters, pots and pans, plus a collection of heavy French copper cookware. Waterford and Baccarat crystal pieces, and drinkware. All kinds of home decor and collectibles from Herend, Limgoes, Meissen, KPM and more. Porcelain figurines collection of bunnies, handpainted Russian boxes, museum quality Ukranian eggs, and many more fun art glass and pottery pieces. Everything is authentic and the real-deal, no knockoffs! And of course they only wore the best designer goods with a primary bedroom full of vintage ladies clothing, designer purses, shoes, and fine silk Hermes scarves. This home is truly a high-end bargain for treasure hunters with something for everyone!

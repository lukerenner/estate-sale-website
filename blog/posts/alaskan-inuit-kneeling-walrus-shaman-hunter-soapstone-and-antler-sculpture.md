Purchased from Douglas Creek Sculpture co., this piece was carved to order and one of a kind. Carved from mottled green-brown soapstone or serpentine stone with lighter stone at the coat trim. The face and spear end  are carved from naturally shed North American antler.

As stated on the site, "_Taking on the spirit of the walrus, an Inuit Shaman is believed to have the power to cast a spell for a successful hunt."_

Measures 6" tall x 4" x 4 1/4"

The spear shows damage where it inserts into hand, as photographed. No other chips, cracks, or repairs.

Incredibly sweet Vintage Ken Edwards Mexican pottery, dinner or charger plate from the El Palomar studio in Tonalá, Mexico.

Crafted in Tonalá, Jalisco, Mexico, this highly collectible high-fire stoneware piece belongs to the popular 'El Palomar Blue' collection. Showcasing iconic folk art elements, including hand-painted stylized blue bird, butterflies, and whimsical floral motifs against a textured pale blue/grey clay background and framed with a earthy cinnamon brown tone rim.

Measures 11 3/4" x 1 1/2" tall.

Excellent condition, no chips, cracks, or repairs.

Signature hallmark at base.

10849-10b

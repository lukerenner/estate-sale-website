A divine Murano art glass sculpture by acclaimed artist Dino Rosin, from his galactic inspired "Pianeta" or Planet series. This ovoid sculpture sits upon an onyx colored cylindrical base, on which the artist's signature can be found. Suspended in the center of a celestial blue void is a pastel toned open conical form that fans out around a transparent bubble. Curving around the sculpture is a lovely opaque glass with a bright color scheme reminiscent of the milky way. This otherworldly art piece is a sight to behold; be sure to place it near a window and watch as the natural light transforms the glass!

Italian artist Dino Rosin was born in Venice in 1948 and raised on the island of Murano, famous for its glass blowing tradition. Rosin left formal schooling to pursue glass blowing at the age of 12. His older brother Loredano Rosin was also a prolific glass artist in Murano, and Dino Rosin joined his workshop in 1975. The Rosin brothers are credited with rediscovering the secrets of Calcedonia glass, a Murano tradition that had been lost for nearly 500 years.

H: 16.5" / W: 11" / D: 6"

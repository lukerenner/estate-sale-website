Southeast Asian rustic bowl, crafted in the distinctive Thailand Sukhothai style. This is an antique piece created to capture the essence of historical Southeast Asian stoneware.

Organic irregular shape, featuring a worn off-white slip accented by faint subtle blue-gray underglaze decoration.

Measures approximately 9 3/4" x 10 1/4" x 3 1/4" tall.

Overall, great condition for old age. Showing obvious wear, as photographed. Glaze is rough and shows heavy wear, but I am not seeing any major cracks or chips. I'm quite fond of the rustic appeal of this bowl. Nice earthen color palette.

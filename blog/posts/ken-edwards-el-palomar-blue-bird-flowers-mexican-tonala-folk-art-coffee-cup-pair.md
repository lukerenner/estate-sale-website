Set of 2 incredibly sweet vintage Ken Edwards Mexican pottery, coffee cups from the El Palomar studio in Tonalá, Mexico.

Crafted in Tonalá, Jalisco, Mexico, this highly collectible high-fire stoneware piece belongs to the popular 'El Palomar Blue' collection. Showcasing iconic folk art elements, including hand-painted stylized blue birds, a butterfly, and whimsical floral motifs against a textured pale blue clay background. The mug is beautifully framed with dark earthy brown accents.

Measures 4" tall x 4 1/2" (including handle) x 3"

Excellent condition, no chips, cracks, or repairs.

Hallmarks at base.

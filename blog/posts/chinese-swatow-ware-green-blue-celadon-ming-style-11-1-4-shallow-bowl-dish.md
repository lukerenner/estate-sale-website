Chinese Swatow ware plate, likely dating from the late Ming Dynasty (16th-17th century). Swatow ware, also known as Zhangzhou ware, is a type of Chinese export porcelain primarily produced in the Zhangzhou region of Fujian province. It is characterized by its somewhat coarse and rustic appearance, like this one featuring a green-blue celadon glaze with decoration underglaze.

Measures 11 1/4" x 2 3/4" tall.

Measures 11 3/8" x 2 3/4" tall

Shows a couple hairlines and chips/wear to glaze, please review photos. Overall, excellent condition for age and this type of pottery.

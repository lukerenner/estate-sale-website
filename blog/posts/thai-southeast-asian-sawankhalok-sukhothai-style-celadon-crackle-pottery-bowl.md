Antique Sawankhalok style lCeladon bowl, originating from Thailand. This piece is in the style of 14th to 15th century Sukhothai era pottery, but I believe it to be a 19th century reproduction.

Rustic stoneware bowl showing signature thick, green crackle glaze and showing an incised diagonal striping around the rim.

Organic, irregular in shape, measures approximately  9 3/4" wide x 2 1/2" tall.

Excellent condition, shows light hairlines in glaze, but not seeing any major chips, cracks, or repairs.

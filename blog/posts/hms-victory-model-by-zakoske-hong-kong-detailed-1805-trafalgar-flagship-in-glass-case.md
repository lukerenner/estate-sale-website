This is an intricately detailed model of the HMS Victory, a wooden sailing ship best known for its role in the Battle of Trafalgar in 1805 under Admiral Horatio Nelson. This model follows a scale of 1/8 inch : 1 foot, and precisely captures the stunning construction of this famous vessel. Displayed in a dignified glass case, this model ship is sure to catch the eye, and to spark conversation with any nautical or military history enthusiasts.

The HMS Victory was first commissioned by the Royal Navy in 1758 and played a role in the American Revolutionary War, the French Revolutionary Wars, and the Napoleonic Wars. The victory secured by Admiral Nelson in the Battle of Trafalgar was a monumental step in making England the greatest sea power of the world at the time. 248 years after its construction, the HMS victory remains in working order today, making it the oldest naval vessel still in commission.

Zakoske (Scale Models) Ltd. was a private company founded in 1973 in Kowloon, Hong Kong. They specialized in making detailed historical model ships for 22 years before dissolving in 1995. Their work remains acclaimed for its precision and historical accuracy.

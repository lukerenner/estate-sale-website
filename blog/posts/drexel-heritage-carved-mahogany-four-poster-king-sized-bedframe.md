A Chippendale or Georgian style four poster king bedframe from renowned furniture company Drexel Heritage. Each post is fluted and topped with a pineapple finial. With ornamental carvings, including the central shell motif on the headboard and foliate details on each post, and ball-and-claw feet on cabriole legs, this bedframe oozes traditional charm. Circular brass accents at each corner provide a final aesthetic flourish.

Box spring not included.

Drexel Heritage has been manufacturing high quality traditional style furniture in the American South since 1903. They are dedicated to creating timeless, reliable pieces that will be treasured for generations.

H: 81" / W: 82" / L: 91"

This is a used vintage item, with signs of wear and tear. The wood has some scratches on the lower frame, but it is unlikely these marks will be particularly noticeable with a mattress in place.

Hand-sculpted Murano art glass cat (_Gatto_) by renowned Italian glassmaster Dino Rosin (b. 1948)

Crafted using Rosin’s signature calcedonia (chalceony) glass technique, this heavy Murano glass sculpture features a vibrant, swirled ribbon of rich crimson, violet, amber, and cobalt blue suspended within thick, optically clear crystal. The streamlined, resting feline form flows seamlessly from its extended front paws to its curved tail, capturing light from every angle.

Measures 13" x 5 1/2" x 3" tall.

Excellent condition, no chips, cracks, or repairs.

Etched signature at lower side.

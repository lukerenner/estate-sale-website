Designed by Thomas Turner, renowned furniture maker to English royalty, it is no surprise this vanity looks fit for a a palace. With its striking silhouette and exquisite marquetry, this Victorian era double pedestal dressing table is the picture of elegance. The central oval tilting mirror rests in an inlaid wooden frame with a molded crown top, adorned with floral motifs and intricate scrollwork. The beveled mirror is flanked by gracefully carved supports, each ornamented with finials and detailed marquetry. Each side of the vanity features four satinwood crossbanded drawers with brass bail pulls for storing jewelry, cosmetics, and clothing pieces. Two small, rounded protruding shelves make excellent surfaces for displaying perfume or sentimental items. The lower tier shelf could serve as a footrest when getting ready, or as additional storage space. Turned legs terminate in casters, making this lovely dressing table relatively easy to move around.

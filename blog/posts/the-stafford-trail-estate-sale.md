Wander down to West Linn and explore the charming Stafford Trail Estate Sale. This large home is full of your favorite household essentials from furniture and decor to kitchen and living accessories. One of those "there's something for everyone" kind of sales with all the useful treasures you'd expect to find at one of our events. It's as if a picture perfect family and all their picture perfect things is now in a picture perfect estate sale!

There's fine art from traditional masterpieces to quirky fun pieces, including Pacific NW artists. Traditional modern furniture from good condition upholstered sofas, loungers, and chairs, to wood dining tables and cabinetry from big American brands like Sherrill, Ethan Allen, Carges, and more. Not to mention classic 4-poster beds. A variety of Asian pottery, decor, and oriental rugs of all sizes. Trendy vintage clothing and footwear full of 80s/90s vibes, plus golfing attire, sweaters, and golf shoes. Costume jewelry and hats. All kinds of kitchenware and bakeware, pots and pans, also lots of glassware, stone pottery, and china. Luggage, bags, briefcases, vintage trunks. Plenty of CDs, electronics, cameras, and office supplies and stationary. Lots of books from children's to historical encyclopedias. Blankets and quilts, but also rolls of upholstery fabric. Christmas decor and so much more!

James Elmo Williams (April 30, 1913 - November 25, 2015) was a film and television editor, producer, director and executive. He lived in Oregon the last 20 years of his life, and when he passed away last fall at 102 he had been the oldest living Oscar winner in the country. He won his Oscar for his work on the film High Noon (1952), receiving the Academy Award for Film Editing. Antique Appraiser Gary Germer joined us today with a few of Elmo's motion picture and personal memorabilia.

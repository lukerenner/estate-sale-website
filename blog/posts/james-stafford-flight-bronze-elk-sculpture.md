Fantastic dynamic bronze sculpture titled "Flight," depicting a majestic Rocky Mountain bull elk mid leap, by Pacific Northwest artist James Stafford. Stafford specializes in realist bronze wildlife sculpture, with a particular focus on North American elk and wild sheep. He is also known for developing his own patinas, which are specific to each bronze.

This sculpture demonstrates Stafford's incredible attention to detail, with every aspect meticulously rendered, from the rocky terrain the elk traverses to its impressive antlers.

Signed J. Stafford "Flight," C 1978, 1/18, with a Malden bronze (Walla Walla, WA) foundry mark. Includes certificate of authenticity.

W: 22"
D: 14"

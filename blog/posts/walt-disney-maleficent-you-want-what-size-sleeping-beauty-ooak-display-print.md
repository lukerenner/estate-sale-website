One of a kind framed Walt Disney print of Disney villainous, Maleficent from _Sleeping Beauty. _ surrounded by lightning bolts and her raven Diablo inside a circular crest, captioned with a playful parody phrase "YOU WANT WHAT SIZE!" at the bottom.

Created by artist Fred Phillips, Disney Costume department. This is a Walt Disney presentation or display print, never in production to be sold, was for internal use only. It was saved and framed by our client who was an artist for Disney World Florida from the late 70's through the 1980's. The client said it was created for a Halloween party in the mid 80's.

Frame measures 29 1/2" x 29 1/2", print measures 24" x 24".

Print is excellent, glass is slightly loose from frame, but intact. Sorry for all the reflections shown in glass.

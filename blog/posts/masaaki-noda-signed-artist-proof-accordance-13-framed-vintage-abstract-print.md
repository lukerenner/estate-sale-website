Masaaki Noda (Japanese/American, 1949), "Accordance-13" signed Artist Proof screen print.

Vibrant framed minimalist screen print blending hard-edge geometrics with gradient, pulsating lines that evoke a sense of optical movement, light emission, and spatial energy.

Marked AP for artist proof, signed, titled, and dated 1973 in pencil at lower.

Simple yellow tone frame measures 15 3/4" x 21 3/4", image measures approximately 13" x 18"

Excellent condition, the photos show reflections in the glass, the print is clear and vivid.

Masaaki Noda was born in Hiroshima Prefecture, Japan, and graduated from the Osaka University of Arts. In 1977, he immigrated to New York City on a scholarship to the Art Students League, seeking to escape restrictive cultural conventions and pursue open artistic expression. While his printmaking, paintings, and large-scale public stainless steel sculptures are deeply rooted in Western abstraction and American Op-Art, his work maintains a unique spiritual response to nature, visual delicacy, and precision reflective of his Japanese heritage

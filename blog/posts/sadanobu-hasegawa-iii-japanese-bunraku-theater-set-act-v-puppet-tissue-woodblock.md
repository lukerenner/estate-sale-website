Hasegawa Sadanobu III (1881–1963), 1920's original Japanese color woodblock print **"**Bunraku Theater Stage Set for Act V, the Yamazaki Highway, from the famous play Kanadehon Chushingura or The Treasury of Loyal Retainers". From his series titled _The Illustrated Collection of Famous Japanese Puppets of the Osaka Bunrakuza. _
As originally issued, this print is complete with its tissue overlay showing the outlines of three puppet characters as they appear on stage.__

This scene presents us with a moonlit night along the Yamazaki highway running between Kyoto and Osaka. Robbery and murder will ensue!

The tissue is intact, but shows creases, as photographed, also a stain at the upper right. The print is unstained, shows light wrinkling from moisture.
Print attached to black matte, print measures 14 5/8" x 8 3/4".

Demonstrating the sublime beauty of simple forms, this Murano glass sculpture by acclaimed artist Dino Rosin depicts a colorful bullseye. The front is composed of concentric circles of translucent blue and purple toned glass, while the back is a mesmerizing swirl of opaque maroon and glowing yellow. The sculpture is affixed to a cylindrical black base signed by the artist. Remarkable from every angle, this lovely work of art is best displayed in natural light.

Italian artist Dino Rosin was born in Venice in 1948 and raised on the island of Murano, famous for its glass blowing tradition. Rosin left formal schooling to pursue glass blowing at the age of 12. His older brother Loredano Rosin was also a prolific glass artist in Murano, and Dino Rosin joined his workshop in 1975. The Rosin brothers are credited with rediscovering the secrets of Calcedonia glass, a Murano tradition that had been lost for nearly 500 years.

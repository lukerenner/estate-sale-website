This darling blown glass angel fish by Dino Rosin is an astonishing example of his traditional Murano glass sculptures. Standing tall and elegant, this work blends opaque and clear glass to create a stunning light-catching effect. On one side of the angel fish you'll observe a dazzling opalescent sheen, while the other side enchants with its lovely hues of pink, red, and orange. This piece of art glass is perfectly suited to be the center of attention, and would fit particularly well in a costal or nautical themed environment.

Italian artist Dino Rosin was born in Venice in 1948 and raised on the island of Murano, famous for its glass blowing tradition. Rosin left formal schooling to pursue glass blowing at the age of 12. His older brother Loredano Rosin was also a prolific glass artist in Murano, and Dino Rosin joined his workshop in 1975. The Rosin brothers are credited with rediscovering the secrets of Calcedonia glass, a Murano tradition that had been lost for nearly 500 years. Dino Rosin's art reflects the traditions of his family and the islands of Murano, and his lifelong passion is evident in this exquisite work of art.

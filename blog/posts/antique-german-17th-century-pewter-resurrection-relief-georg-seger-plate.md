17th Century German pewter resurrection plate with eagle touch mark, Nuremberg relief pewter plate. Measures 7 inches (18cm) diameter.

7 figures around the edges. In the center the risen Christ with Roman soldiers and signature "CHRISTVS - IST - AVF - STANDEN - VON - VON - DFM -DOT ", on the flag seven fields with the enthroned Emperor behind the double eagle coat of arms and the six electors behind their coats of arms between vase ornaments, Type III with masks and fruit hangings and half eagle and rafter with saw mark of Georg Seger (master 1622).

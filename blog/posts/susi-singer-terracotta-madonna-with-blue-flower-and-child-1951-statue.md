Susi Singer (1891-1955, Austrian/American, AKA Selma Singer-Schinnerl) large terracotta Madonna and Child statue.

This piece features a beautifully expressive, hand-painted, and glazed terracotta/earthenware depiction of the Virgin Mary holding the Christ child. In her left hand, she delicately holds a single blue flower, showcasing Singer's characteristic touch of adding gentle, whimsical natural elements. The sculpture emits a serene, peaceful aura.

Signed and dated 1951 at lower back.

Measures approximately 15" tall x 10" x 5"

Excellent condition, found no cracks or repairs. Rough edges to glaze, may be some chips along lower rim. All 'flaws' (or not) are seemingly natural parts of the terracotta pottery

As found on SusanneBauer site:

SINGER SUSI

**(Vienna 1891 – New York 1955)**

Susi Singer was an Austrian ceramic artist who became known primarily for her colourful, expressive figures. After training in Vienna, she worked for the Wiener Werkstätte from 1917 onwards, where she developed her own unique style. In the 1920s, she established her own workshop in Grünbach, where she created numerous distinctive ceramics. In 1938, she emigrated to the United States, where she continued her artistic work. Singer's works combine traditional craftsmanship with individual expressiveness – playful, rich in detail and unmistakable.

Gorgeous cedar lined chest by Cavalier featuring hand carved ornamental accents, a lovely walnut veneer, and turned bolster feet. With its spacious interior, this piece is a versatile and striking storage solution that would serve well as a linens chest, a place for storing books and games, or even an eye catching coffee table.

No key is included; the lock is a pressure based mechanism.

The Cavalier Corporation emerged from the Tennessee Furniture Company, founded in 1865 in Chattanooga, Tennessee. In 1923, the Tennessee Furniture Company adopted the Cavalier name for their newly released line of cedar chests and soda coolers. Cavalier cedar chests like this one were primarily manufactured from 1923 to the 1940s. The original Cavalier brass emblem can be found inside of the chest on the lid.

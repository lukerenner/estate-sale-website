About the Sale

Practically New, Top to Bottom

Venture up into Forest Heights and you’ll find a large home full of furniture and fine art that’s barely been lived with. The owners had just finished furnishing the house — new Restoration Hardware sofas, chairs, and tables throughout, alongside vintage McGuire and Baker pieces — before a sudden passing left it all behind. There’s no rubble to sort through here; everything is in excellent, like-new condition.

The art and objects are just as strong as the furniture. Look for fine art from popular Pacific Northwest painters Sally Haley and Charles Heaney, alongside a collection of Chinese porcelain and Asian pottery.

Round out the sale with vintage clothing and handbags, toys, board and card games, collectible dolls, a ping pong table and NordicTrack treadmill, and a full kitchen’s worth of pots, pans, dishes, and silverware.
